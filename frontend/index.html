WHO WE ARE

All About the Climate Action

Climate Action is a community-focused initiative that aims to spread awareness about climate change and encourage people to adopt sustainable lifestyles.

We believe that meaningful change starts with awareness. Through education, community participation and technology, we want to help individuals understand their environmental impact and take practical steps towards reducing it.

Our website provides a carbon footprint calculator that helps users estimate emissions from electricity consumption and daily transportation.
